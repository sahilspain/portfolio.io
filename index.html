About me

I build the layer where human intention meets machine autonomy — fleets of marine robots that self-organise, coordinate, and understand plain language.

As a PhD Candidate at Universidad Politécnica de Madrid (UPM), I work on the project “Autoorganización y colaboración de vehículos submarinos empleando inteligencia artificial” (Ref: PID2023-146540OB-C42) — developing and implementing coordinated navigation methods for marine environments.

My research integrates software simulation with hardware testing. With a strong foundation in Python and statistical theory, I'm dedicated to optimising autonomous swarm behaviours in robotics — and to pushing AI innovations that shape the future of marine autonomy.
